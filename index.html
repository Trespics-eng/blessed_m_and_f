A Blessing Called
Mother & Father

Dedicated with Endless Love

🌹 Mother, you carried me before I knew the light,

You held me through the days and through the night.

Your gentle hands that wiped my tears away

Taught me that love grows deeper every day.

You gave up sleep, you gave up rest,

You gave your heart, you gave your best.

Your prayers went up when I could not pray,

And lifted me along life’s way.

🍷 Father, you stood like a mountain strong,

Showing me where I truly belong.

You worked through sun and worked through rain,

Carrying burdens and hiding your pain.

You taught me to walk with courage and pride,

With honesty and truth always at my side.

Your quiet sacrifice, your steady hand,

Helped me rise and helped me stand.

🕊️ How blessed I am to call you mine,

Two precious gifts, a love divine.

For every meal and every lesson learned,

For every sacrifice that you have earned,

I thank you, with all that I can give,

For showing me how to truly live.

✨ So may God bless you, and keep you near,

May He wipe away each worry and fear.

May His peace surround you, His joy be your song,

And may His mercy guide you all life long.

May He be with you in every season,

Give you health, and a heart at peace with reason.

And may the love you gave so freely to me

Return to you, abundantly.

💖 Mother and Father, my gift from above,

I am forever grateful for your love.

💌

Thank you for everything you have done in my life.

With all my heart & soul

Crafted with love for Mom & Dad • ❤️
